# FAQ
## Reputation
### The numbers in my reputation details look strange
They are not the same figures as on your other screens. Your reputation looks back over the **last 12 weeks**, with recent days counting more, while the Aircraft and Personnel screens show how things are today. So a change you just made takes a while to show in your reputation, and a bad stretch a few weeks ago still holds it back. See [When it changes](reputation.md#when-it-changes).

**Aircraft condition is lower (or higher) than on the Aircraft screen.** The Aircraft screen shows each aircraft's condition today, and counts every aircraft once, including those on the ground. Your reputation counts only the aircraft that flew, in the condition they flew in, for every passenger on board. Your busiest, fullest aircraft count the most, and they are often the most worn. If you have recently raised your maintenance or overhauled aircraft, the reputation figure catches up over the following weeks.

**Satisfaction and competence don't match what my staff look like now.** Both are averages over every employee across the 12 weeks, not today's roster. Competence leaves specialists out.

**Turnover seems high for the few people who left.** Turnover is the share of your staff who left, quit or fired, in the **last 4 weeks** only. Someone who left yesterday counts in full and fades out over the 4 weeks. If 1 person out of 100 left yesterday, it reads 1%, and half that two weeks later.

**My score did not change after I fixed something.** Your score is worked out once a night, so nothing you do during the day shows until the next morning. Even then it moves gradually, because one good day is a small part of 12 weeks.

## Development
### A screen says "Needs" something
That feature is still locked. The name after **Needs** is the node of the development tree that unlocks it: tap **Development** next to it to go to the tree and buy it. See [Development](development.md).

### I did something, but did not get its achievement
Achievements are checked once a night, so anything you do during the day is paid the next morning, in the morning report. Some achievements also need a node first, which the Achievements tab tells you. See [How they work](achievements.md#how-they-work).
