# Leaderboard
The leaderboard ranks every airline in your game by what it is worth, with how many passengers each has flown and its reputation beside it. Under the table are the game's records and the airlines that have been taken over.

Open it from the menu at the top of the screen, under **Leaderboard**. It is there in every game, even with no other airline in it yet.

Everything on it is public. Every airline in the game sees the same leaderboard, and none of it needs an [informer](personnel/jobs/informer.md).

## The table
**One row for every airline in the game**, yours included, with computer airlines among the rest. A computer airline's CEO is drawn as a robot. A rival that has filed is not listed until its launch day. An airline that has been taken over moves to [Closed](#closed).

**Ranked by company value**, highest first. Airlines on the same value share a rank. An airline that has no value yet, because it has not had its first night, comes last with no rank. The headings do not sort the table.

**The figures are as of the end of yesterday**, and the line under the title says which day that is. They change once a night.

Your row is highlighted and marked **you**.

- **Company value**: what the airline is worth (see [below](#company-value)).
- **Pax**: every passenger the airline has flown since its first day, which is shown under it. Yours starts on the first day of the game; a rival's on its launch day. An airline you absorb brings its aircraft and its crew, but not its passengers: the count is only ever your own flights.
- **Reputation**: the airline's [reputation](reputation.md) score. A new airline reads **No score yet** until its first night has scored it.

On a phone, the value sits beside the airline's name, and the passengers and their first day are on a line of their own.

## Company value
Company value is what an airline is worth: what it owns less what it owes, plus what it has been earning lately (a loss counts against it), with a little for its reputation and its incidents. It is the figure an airline's share price follows (see [The price](finance/shares.md#the-price)).

**It is not the share price**, nor the **Market value** on the Shares screen, which is the price times every share. Those move with the market's mood and with each day's trading. Company value does not, so buying or selling shares never moves anyone up or down the table.

If a rival [takes you over](finance/shares.md#being-taken-over), the stock market closes for good, and every company value stays at the figure it had that day. The passengers and the scores keep moving.

## Records
The best anyone has done in the game, each with the airline that holds it. A record set by an airline that has since been taken over still stands.

| Record | What it is | On a tie |
|---|---|---|
| **Busiest day** | the most passengers one airline flew in a single day, and the day | the earlier day |
| **Best reputation** | the highest score one airline was given on a night, and the day | the earlier day |
| **Most achievements** | the most [achievements](achievements.md) one airline has earned, each step of a ladder counting one | whoever got there first |
| **Takeovers** | the most airlines one airline has [absorbed or liquidated](finance/shares.md#control) | whoever got there first |

Computer airlines earn no achievements, so Most achievements is always held by a player.

A record nobody holds yet is not shown, and nor is the section until somebody holds one.

## Closed
Every airline that has been absorbed or liquidated, the most recent first, with who took it over and when, for example **Absorbed by Copperleaf Air · Nov 9, Year 2** or **Liquidated by Gullwing · Mar 3, Year 3**.

The section appears once the first airline has closed. See [Absorbing](finance/shares.md#absorbing) and [Liquidating](finance/shares.md#liquidating).
