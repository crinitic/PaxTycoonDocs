# Development
A new airline cannot do everything at once. Most of what makes an airline big, like widebodies, lounges, extra gates and rented routes, is locked at the start of a game, and you unlock it one step at a time on your **development tree**.

Open it from the menu at the top of the screen, under **Development**. It has two tabs: **Achievements**, where you earn development points, and **Tree**, where you spend them.

## Development points
Development points (**DP**) come from [achievements](achievements.md), and from nothing else. Money cannot buy them, and nothing you unlock costs money.

Your balance is at the top of the Development dialog, and next to **Development** in the menu.

## The tree
The tree is drawn in four groups: **Fleet**, **Airport**, **Routes** and **HQ**. The whole tree is there from your first day, including everything you cannot buy yet, so you can plan ahead.

Each node is one of:

- **Bought**: unlocked for the rest of the game.
- **Affordable**: you have the DP to buy it.
- **Too expensive**: you need more DP.
- **Needs another node first**: the node it hangs under in the tree. Buy that one first.

Tap a node to see what it unlocks and what it costs, then **Buy** it.

**What you buy is yours for good.** A node is never taken away, and DP spent cannot be taken back.

### Fleet
| Node | DP | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Configure cabin | 5 | Choosing the cabin layout of a new aircraft, and changing the layout of one you own. |
| Premium service | 20 | 2★ and 3★ seats and entertainment, and Meal and Feast [catering](routes/catering.md). |
| Regional | 25 | Buying regional aircraft. |
| Widebody | 50 | Buying widebody aircraft. |

You start with narrowbodies. The catalogue and the used market list regional and widebody aircraft from the start, marked with the node they need.

**Before Configure cabin**, a new aircraft comes with your home airport's usual layout, and a used one keeps the layout its last owner gave it.

**Before Premium service**, a new aircraft comes with 1★ seats and 1★ entertainment. You can still buy a used aircraft with 2★ or 3★ seats or entertainment, and it keeps them, even when you change its layout.

### Airport
| Node | DP | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra gates | 5 | | Renting more than one [gate](airport/gates.md). |
| Terminals | 40 | Extra gates | Building Terminal B and the terminals after it. |
| Basic lounges | 15 | | Building [lounges](airport/lounges.md), and hiring [lounge staff](personnel/jobs/lounge-staff.md). |
| Advanced lounges | 40 | Basic lounges | Deluxe and Palace lounges. |

Terminals only gives you the right to build: a terminal still costs what it costs.

A lounge at the far end of a rented route also needs that route, so in practice it needs Direct rentals too.

### Routes
Seasonal offers are always open, whatever you have bought.

| Node | DP | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct rentals | 15 | | Renting a route with your home airport at one end. |
| Indirect X→Y | 50 | Direct rentals | Renting a route between two airports you already serve from home. |
| Anywhere → anywhere | 250 | Indirect X→Y | Renting any route in the game. |

**Indirect X→Y** lets you rent a route between two airports X and Y when you already hold a seasonal offer or a rented route from home to X, and another from home to Y. Your aircraft can then fly home → X → Y, and spend the night at Y.

**Anywhere → anywhere** drops that condition: any two airports, whether you serve them or not.

When you look at a route to rent, its screen tells you which node it needs, if any.

### HQ
Skyway Robbery Finance always lends, whatever you have bought.

No computer airline trades shares until you have **Shares**, so until then nobody can take you over. See [The Shares node](finance/shares.md#the-shares-node).

| Node | DP | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Security manager | 10 | Hiring a [security manager](personnel/jobs/security-manager.md). |
| Lawyer | 15 | Hiring a [lawyer](personnel/jobs/lawyer.md). |
| Trainer | 10 | Hiring a [trainer](personnel/jobs/trainer.md), and so [training](personnel/training.md). |
| Better lenders | 20 | Borrowing from Holding Pattern, Quickturn and Throttlebury. |
| Fuel management | 25 | Buying [fuel tanks](hq/fuel.md#tanks). |
| Shares | 50 | Buying and selling [shares](finance/shares.md) in any airline, and taking over an airline you control. |

## What is never locked
Seasonal offers, Skyway Robbery Finance, selling aircraft, overhauls, firing staff, the fuel price and its history, and the share prices and their history are open to everyone from the first day.

Going down is never locked either. You can always release a gate, lower a lounge's level, choose cheaper catering, or fit 1★ seats, whatever you have bought.

## When something is locked
A screen that offers something you have not unlocked says so, and names the node it needs, for example **Needs Widebody**. Tap **Development** next to it to go straight to the tree. When you come back after buying the node, the lock is gone.

## How much there is
The whole tree costs 645 DP. Achievements pay out about 1,080 DP in all, so you can finish the tree without earning every achievement. Skip the ones that do not suit how you play, and do harder ones instead.

There is no order to buy the tree in. Price alone sets the pace: the cheap nodes come first because you can afford them first.

## Everything unlocked
When you set up a new game, you can tick **Everything unlocked**. Every feature is then open from the first day, for every airline in the game, and there are no achievements and no development tree. It is off by default, and cannot be changed once the game has started.

In a game with everything unlocked, there is no **Development** in the menu.

Games started before the development tree came to PAX Tycoon have everything unlocked.
