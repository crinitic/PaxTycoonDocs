# Mechanic
Mechanics keep the fleet serviceable. Unlike everyone else you hire in bulk, headcount and competence do two separate jobs here.

Each mechanic can only tend so many aeroplanes. Once your fleet outgrows your mechanics, the aircraft nobody reaches are worse off than the ones in your *worst* mechanic's hands. An uncovered aeroplane is the single worst state an aircraft can be in. Cover the fleet before you worry about quality. Ten mediocre mechanics covering the whole fleet beat one brilliant one who can only reach a fraction of it.
