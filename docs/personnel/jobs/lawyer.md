# Lawyer
A lawyer is who you want when an employee causes an incident. They do not stop incidents happening, that is competence and satisfaction, but they decide how much the aftermath costs you.

The most expensive person on this list, and the one you notice least. An airline that never needs its lawyer has been paying one for nothing, which is the correct outcome.

Only your best lawyer counts.
