# Informer
An informer tells you what your [rivals](../../competitors.md) are doing: where they fly and what they charge, what they fly it with, how full their flights are, and how they are doing for money. They also hear about rivals' Moderate incidents, which never make the news.

The [News](../../intel/news.md) is free and needs nobody. Everything else you learn about a rival comes from your informer.

Only your best informer counts.

## Competence
The better your informer, the more they find out, and each level finds out everything the level below it does.

- **Disaster**: Each rival's routes: the days it flies each one, its departure times from both ends, and its fares in each cabin.
- **Questionable**: Adds each rival's fleet: which aircraft types it flies, and how many of each.
- **Competent**: Adds loads: how full each rival's flights on each route were over the last 7 days.
- **Impressive**: Adds finances: each rival's balance, its revenue over the last 28 days, and what it owes on loans.
- **Exceptional**: Everything an Impressive informer finds out. Watching, where you tell your informer what to look out for, is coming later.

The game never tells you what the next level up would find out. Whatever your informer cannot reach is simply not shown.

## Leaks
Read what your informer finds out from **Intel > Leaks**. The Intel tile counts your rivals that are already flying.

Without an informer, Leaks only tells you to hire one. With one, it lists every rival in your game, in the order they arrived, with its CEO and its score. A rival that has filed but not started flying yet is marked **Filed**, and has nothing to show until its first flights. Tap a flying rival to see as much of it as your informer finds out. Departure times are in your home airport's time, as on your own flight board.

Under the rivals are the leaked incidents.

## On the route screen
A route's screen shows who else flies it. Your informer gives each rival's days, departures and fares on that route, and from **Competent** how full its seats were over the last 7 days. Tap the rival's name to open everything else your informer knows about it.

Without an informer, the route screen only tells you to hire one.

## Leaked incidents
Every Moderate incident another airline causes has a chance of reaching your informer, and the better your informer, the better the chance: a Disaster hears about very few, and an Exceptional one about nearly all of them. Each leak is listed under Leaks, under the day it happened, worded as a news story would be, and stays there for **12 weeks**.

Minor incidents never leak. Major ones make the [News](../../intel/news.md), so everyone knows about them anyway.

Computer airlines do not hire informers. Unless there are other players in your game, nobody hears about your own Moderate incidents. See [Incidents](../absence-incidents.md#incidents).
