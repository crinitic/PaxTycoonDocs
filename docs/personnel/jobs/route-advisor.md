# Route Advisor
A route advisor shows where the passengers are. Every route has a season, a rhythm through the week and an hour of the day it fills up, and a route advisor is who works that out and draws it for you.

You don't need one to size a route: every airline sees each route's average passengers a day over the year, and how they split across the cabins. An advisor shows how that average moves.

What you are buying is sight rather than judgement. Nothing an advisor tells you is wrong. A poor one simply sees less of the picture, and the part they cannot see is not drawn at all.

Only your best route advisor counts.

## Competence

- **Disaster**: Shares real but unhelpful facts about the cities at each end of the route.
- **Questionable**: Draws the hours of the day, so you can see when people like to leave, with the morning and evening rushes named and the night shaded. Every route shares the same curve, and it shows when, not how many. When you schedule a flight, the same curve sits above the departure time and shows what the flight would reach that your other departures on the route that day don't.
- **Competent**: Adds a route's season, in passengers a day: the months it fills and the months it empties. Each month is split into First, Business and Economy, and the current month's passengers in each cabin are written out under the chart. Also tells you how many aircraft in your fleet can fly that route, and **See aircraft** lists them: each one's type, cabin and range, with the ones that cannot reach it apart and how far short they fall. Tap one to open it.
- **Impressive**: Adds the week in passengers a day, so you can see which days carry a route and which do not. It is split by cabin the same way, with today's weekday written out. On a route you rent, also finds routes that pair well with it: a season opposite to this one, on a similar cabin mix, out of the airports you already fly to.
- **Exceptional**: Shows how your fares change demand. It works this out as if nobody else flew the route. Where [rivals](../../competitors.md) fly it too, being better value than them can win you more than it says, and worse value can lose you more.

## In the Seats section
A route's [Seats](../../routes/seats.md) section shows the seats flying it on a given day, and your advisor adds to it:

- **Questionable**: Draws that day's curve with what your departures reach, and any busy stretch they leave.
- **Competent**: Puts the day's seats against its passengers, on an average day in that month.
- **Impressive**: Puts them against that date's passengers exactly, weekday included.
- **Exceptional**: Prices that date's passengers at your fares on the route that day.
