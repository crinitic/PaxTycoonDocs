# Aircraft Advisor
An aircraft advisor appraises the used market. Every second-hand airframe carries a price its seller chose, and this is the person who tells you what it is actually worth.

They only ever give you an opinion. The ask is the ask, and it does not move because your advisor disapproves of it. Arguing the price down is somebody else's job.

A new aeroplane off the catalogue never needs appraising, every airline in the game pays the same list price for one, so there is nothing to judge. Neither does anything you already own: appraisal is only ever about what a stranger's asking price is worth.

An aircraft advisor also tells you what [age](../../aircraft/age.md) is doing to your own fleet.

Only your best aircraft advisor counts.

- **Disaster**: Values everything at roughly what it is being sold for.
- **Questionable**: Catches the worst of the market and misses the rest.
- **Competent**: A usable read on whether an ask is fair.
- **Impressive**: Prices an airframe closely enough to bid against it.
- **Exceptional**: Tells you which airframe on the market is the bargain, and by how much.

## Aircraft age
Everyone sees an aircraft's hours, its age word and the hours it has left before it is scrapped. How much faster it wears because of its age, and when it will retire, is your advisor's to say. They say it on the aircraft's **Condition** tab, and in one line about the whole fleet on the **Aircraft** screen.

They say nothing about an aircraft that is still young: under about 27,000 hours, age barely matters.

- **Disaster** and **Questionable**: Say nothing about age.
- **Competent**: Say how much faster an old aircraft wears, in words: slightly, noticeably or much. On the Aircraft screen, how many of your aircraft are wearing faster with age.
- **Impressive**: Give the figure, for example 1.88× faster than a young airframe. On the Aircraft screen, the figure for the oldest.
- **Exceptional**: Also say how many hours a day the aircraft is flying, from its schedule, and in about how many days it will reach the limit at that rate. On the Aircraft screen, which aircraft retires next, and when.
