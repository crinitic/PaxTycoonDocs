# Aircraft Advisor
An aircraft advisor appraises the used market. Every second-hand airframe carries a price its seller chose, and this is the person who tells you what it is actually worth.

They only ever give you an opinion. The ask is the ask, and it does not move because your advisor disapproves of it. Arguing the price down is somebody else's job.

A new aeroplane off the catalogue never needs appraising, every airline in the game pays the same list price for one, so there is nothing to judge. Neither does anything you already own: appraisal is only ever about what a stranger's asking price is worth.

Only your best aircraft advisor counts.

## Competence

- **Disaster**: Values everything at roughly what it is being sold for.
- **Questionable**: Catches the worst of the market and misses the rest.
- **Competent**: A usable read on whether an ask is fair.
- **Impressive**: Prices an airframe closely enough to bid against it.
- **Exceptional**: Tells you which airframe on the market is the bargain, and by how much.
