# Flight Attendant
Attendants work the cabin, and how many an aeroplane needs depends on the aeroplane. Like pilots they are assigned to an aircraft, and like pilots a shortage grounds it.

## Competence
!!! warning "Pending Implementation"
    Competence doesn't have an impact on passenger satisfaction yet.

Low competence attendants decrease the passenger satisfaction on a flight, and have higher chances of starting incidents. See [Incidents](../absence-incidents.md#incidents).

## Satisfaction
- Miserable
- Unhappy
- Okay
- Happy
- Delighted

Low satisfaction increases the likelihood of:

- Employee quitting
- Employee being sick
- Employee being the cause of an incident

A raise is the only thing that lifts satisfaction. See [Satisfaction](../salary-increases.md).
