# Financial Advisor
A financial advisor reads the [stock market](../../finance/shares.md) for you. Their job is to tell you when control of a rival is within your reach: when what the public has available to buy, added to what you already hold, comes to more than half of it.

With one, a rival's card on **Finance > Shares** says so, for example:

> Available to buy: 38%. With your 15%, that's 53%: enough for control.

They only speak up when there is enough. A rival out of your reach gets no line, so they never tell you that there is not enough, and never who holds the rest. From Competent up, your advisor heads the Shares screen while the market is open.

Only your best financial advisor counts.

## Competence
- **Disaster**: Nothing yet.
- **Questionable**: Nothing yet.
- **Competent**: Tells you when control of a rival is within reach.
- **Impressive**: The same as a Competent advisor.
- **Exceptional**: The same as a Competent advisor.

Without an advisor, or with one below Competent, the market shows how much of each rival is available to buy, and you work out the rest yourself.
