# Trainer
A trainer is what makes training possible at all. Without one on the payroll there is no course to put anybody on, and every employee you have is stuck with the ability they arrived with.

The trainer is also the rare job where you are buying nothing but the competence. Their own rating sets how fast every course in your airline moves, for everybody, at once.

Only your best trainer counts. A second one is payroll.

## Competence

- **Disaster**: Courses move so slowly that the roster gap will cost you more than the improvement is worth.
- **Questionable**: Better than nothing. Expect to leave people on a course for months to see change.
- **Competent**: A workable pace: a month or so of somebody's time to move them up a band.
- **Impressive**: Fast enough that training becomes a routine part of running the airline rather than a project.
- **Exceptional**: Worth waiting for, and worth overpaying for when one appears.
