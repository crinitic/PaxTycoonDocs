# Security Manager
A security manager argues about money on your behalf when your airline is buying new aircraft off the catalogue, used ones off the market, and fuel tanks.

They show you nothing and change nothing about what you see beforehand: the whole of their work turns up as a smaller number on the receipt, after the deal is done, never as a quote you could shop around with.

Aircraft and fuel tanks are the only things they negotiate. Gates, terminals, lounges and the fuel itself cost the same whoever you employ.

They only ever work the buying side. What your airline sells goes to the same buyer for the same cut whoever is negotiating. And they strike one deal per order, not one per aeroplane, so three identical aircraft bought together are billed at the same price rather than three separate rolls of the dice.

Only your best security manager counts.

Hiring one needs **Security manager** from the [development tree](../../development.md).

## Competence

- **Disaster**: Negotiates nothing at all. You pay exactly what you would have paid with nobody in the job.
- **Questionable**: Shaves a little off, and not reliably.
- **Competent**: A dependable discount, worth noticing on a single aircraft.
- **Impressive**: Enough off a fleet order to be most of the reason you hired one.
- **Exceptional**: The best discount anybody in the game can negotiate, on every single order.
