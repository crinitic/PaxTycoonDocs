# Training
Training is how an employee gets better at their job. You send someone on a course, a [Trainer](jobs/trainer.md) teaches them, and their competence climbs a little every night until you bring them back.

## What a course costs
A course costs no money. Sending someone and bringing them back are free, and they are paid as normal while they are away.

What it costs is the person. Someone on a course is not at work, so for as long as they are there, you are a pilot, a mechanic or an advisor short. That gap in your roster is the price of training, and the longer the course, the higher it is.

A trained employee keeps their new competence at the pay they already had. Training never raises anyone's pay.

## Who can go
Anyone you employ who is at work. Not someone who is off sick, and not someone already on a course.

You need a trainer. Without one, nobody can be sent. Hiring a trainer needs **Trainer** from the [development tree](../development.md). Your last trainer cannot be sent either, because then nobody would be left to teach. With two trainers, either one can go, and the other teaches them.

## Sending someone
Open the person's page under **Personnel** and tap **Train**, between **Raise** and **Fire**. The dialog says what happens next. If nobody is there to teach them, it says so, and you need to hire a trainer first.

A pilot or flight attendant comes off their aircraft as soon as they are sent, and a spare from the bench takes their place. If you have no spare, the aircraft is a crew member short and its flights are cancelled until somebody is back or you hire. See [Absence and Incidents](absence-incidents.md).

## While they are away
Someone on a course is not at work:

- pilots and flight attendants do not fly;
- mechanics tend no aircraft, and lounge staff do not count towards your lounges, so a lounge can close;
- advisors stop advising until they are back. Send your personnel advisor, and their opinions on training are gone until they return;
- nobody on a course causes an incident or falls ill.

Their satisfaction still falls as it does at work, you can still give them a raise, and they can still quit.

Anyone on a course still counts in your headcount, since you still pay them. The spare or short count beside it only counts the people at work, the same as for anyone off sick. A line such as **6 pilots · 1 in training · 1 spare** means you employ 6 pilots, 1 of them is on a course, and the 5 at work are 1 more than your fleet needs.

## How fast they learn
Your best trainer sets the pace, for everyone on a course at once. A Competent trainer takes about a month to move someone up a band, an Exceptional one a little over two weeks, and a Disaster several months. See [Trainer](jobs/trainer.md).

Everyone has a limit, their **potential**, and nobody improves past it. Many people are already at their limit the day you hire them, and a course does nothing for them at all. Nobody shows you anyone's potential. A [Personnel Advisor](jobs/personnel-advisor.md) can tell you, before you send someone, whether training them would be worth it.

## The Training list
The Personnel hub has a **Training** tile. It opens the list of everyone on a course, with how long each of them has been there.

Your trainer is at the top of the list. If you have none, it says nobody is running the course, and **Find one →** takes you to Hiring, showing trainers.

### Reached potential
When someone has stopped improving, your trainer notices, and their row is marked **Reached potential**. From then on, every day they spend on the course is wasted, so it is time to bring them back.

The mark does not appear the moment they stop. Your trainer has to watch them for a while first, and a better trainer notices sooner. An Exceptional trainer notices in a few days, a Competent one in about a week, and a Disaster in over a month. Hiring a better trainer brings a mark forward.

The mark is only on the Training list. It is not on the person's page or in the morning report.

## Bringing them back
Nobody comes back on their own, marked or not. You bring people back one at a time, with **Return**, either on their row in the Training list or on their own page, where it takes the place of **Train**.

A pilot or flight attendant who comes back goes to the bench, or straight onto an aircraft that is short.

If you send the same person again later, your trainer starts watching them afresh, and any mark they had is gone.

## If your trainer leaves
If your last trainer quits or is fired, everyone on a course stays there, but nobody learns anything and nobody is watching, so the marks go too. A trainer who is off sick or on a course teaches nobody either.

You can bring people back without a trainer. Or hire one, and the courses carry on.
