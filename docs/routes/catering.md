# Catering
Every route you hold, rented or seasonal, has a catering level: **Snack**, **Meal** or **Feast**. It is set on the route's screen and applies to both directions and every cabin. New routes start on Snack.

## Why it matters
A better meal makes your flights on that route more attractive to passengers, the same way better seats do. Snack is neutral, Meal gives a small edge and Feast a bigger one.

## Cost
You pay for every passenger you carry, and the price depends on the route's distance.

The route's screen shows the price for each cabin and roughly what the level costs a day.

## Changing the level
Pick a level and save it. It applies from tomorrow's flights: today's flights keep the level they already had.

## In the ledger
Catering is paid every night for all your routes together, as one **Catering** line under **The night**.
