# Past offers
**Past offers** lists every seasonal offer you took that has since ended, and how much you flew it. It opens from the button next to the back arrow on the **Routes** screen. It only shows: nothing on it changes your routes or your schedule.

## Which offers it lists
An offer ends when its last day is over, whether you flew it or not. Its days start the day after you took it, so a 28-day offer runs for the 28 days from then. Offers still running stay on the **Offers** screen.

## What a card shows
- **How many of its days flew**, out of the days it ran, in the corner and as a bar. A day counts when at least one flight on the offer flew that day. A cancelled flight does not count. Both turn green when every day flew, which is what the [Every day](../achievements.md#basics) achievement asks for.
- **Passengers** carried, and how full its flights were. Both count only the flights that flew. An offer you never flew reads **Never flown**.
- **Taken**, the day you took it, and **Ended**, its last day.

Flights that moved to a rental, when you rented the route during the offer, are no longer the offer's, so they count for neither the days nor the passengers.

Tap a card to open its route.

The fee is not shown here. It is in the ledger, on the day you took the offer.

## Summary and order
The box at the top counts every offer that has ended, and the passengers flown on them.

Below it, order them **Newest first** (by the day you took them) or **Most passengers**.

If no offer has ended yet, the screen says so, and stays empty until one does.
