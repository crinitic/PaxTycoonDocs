# Fares
A flight's fare is two things together: its direction's **route fare**, and the flight's own **offset** from it. Both are set in [Your routes](your-routes.md), and nowhere else.

## The route fare
Each direction of a route you hold has its own route fare in each cabin, First, Business and Economy. LHR → CDG and CDG → LHR are priced apart, because their passengers are.

Set it with **Change fares**, on the direction's open row in Your routes. It shows the three fares, with the seats each cabin flies that way a week, and says how many flights it changes. Tick **Also CDG → LHR** to set the way back to the same fares in the one go.

A typed fare is moved onto the cabin's step when you leave the box: the step is the largest round amount within a twentieth of the cabin's **Fare** on the route's screen, so ¤10 on a ¤208 economy fare and ¤50 on a ¤1,249 first one.

A new fare changes every weekly flight that way, and every once-only flight still to fly, from tomorrow. Today's flights are locked.

### A new direction
The first flight you schedule in a direction starts its route fare on the **Fare** the route's screen shows for each cabin, put onto its step. Change it in Your routes from then on.

The route fare stays yours when you lose the route. Hold it again and its flights start on the fares you left.

The **Fare** on the route's screen is the route's, the same for every airline. Your route fare is yours. [Reputation](../reputation.md) and [rivals](../competitors.md) measure fares against the route's.

## A flight's offset
Each flight can sit above or below its route fare by a whole percent, from **−50%** to **+100%**. Step it 5% at a time with − and +, or type in any whole percent. A new flight starts at 0%.

Set it on the flight's card in Your routes. It applies from the flight's next flight, and the card says which day that is.

One offset covers every cabin on the flight. What makes one flight worth more or less than the others on a route is when it leaves and how good its aircraft is, and both of those move every cabin together.

## What a flight charges
A flight charges its route fare moved by its offset, put onto the cabin's step. With an economy route fare of ¤84 on a ¤2 step:

| Offset | Moved | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | ¤84 | ¤84 |
| −10% | ¤75.60 | ¤76 |
| +15% | ¤96.60 | ¤96 |

A cabin with a route fare of nothing stays at nothing, whatever the offset.

The offset follows the route fare. Raise the route fare and every flight that way moves with it: a flight at +15% stays 15% above the rest.

The flight editor sets no fare. It shows what the flight charges in each cabin it has seats in, and the route fare and offset under it where it has an offset. **Change in Your routes ›** opens Your routes on that route and direction.

## With a route advisor
With an **Exceptional** [route advisor](../personnel/jobs/route-advisor.md), **Change fares** says what the fares you type do to each cabin's demand, for a flight at the route fare, and a flight's card says the same for the fare its offset gives.
