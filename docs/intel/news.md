# News
News is what every airline in your game can read about every other, and about yours. When an airline does something worth talking about, good or bad, it makes the news, and stays there for **12 weeks**. So does every shock in the price of fuel.

Read it from **Intel > News**. You do not need to hire anyone for it. The Intel screen shows the latest story, and the News tile counts the stories of the last 4 weeks.

## What makes the news
| Story | When | Reads like |
|---|---|---|
| Incidents | an employee causes a **Major** incident | A Gossip Air gate agent let a goat on as carry-on. |
| Groundings | an aircraft becomes too worn to fly | A Gossip Air Boeing 737-800 was grounded, too worn to fly. |
| Layoffs | an airline fires more than 25 employees in 4 weeks | Gossip Air laid off 27 employees in 28 days. |
| Terminals | an airline opens a new terminal | Gossip Air opened its second terminal. |
| Routes | an airline starts flying somewhere new | Gossip Air is now flying to Nashville. |
| Fuel | the price of fuel jumps overnight | A refinery fire on the Gulf Coast sent jet fuel up 18% overnight. |

**Incidents.** Only Major incidents make the news. Minor and Moderate ones stay between you and your passengers. The story names the airline and the job, never the employee, so your rivals never learn who your staff are. See [Incidents](../personnel/absence-incidents.md#incidents).

**Groundings.** An aircraft is grounded when its condition falls too low to fly. The story names the aircraft type, never its registration. If you overhaul an aircraft and let it wear out again, it makes the news again.

**Layoffs.** Only employees you fire count, not those who quit. Once an airline makes the news for layoffs, it does not do so again for 4 weeks, however many more it fires.

**Terminals.** Every terminal after the first one, which comes with your base. See [Gates](../airport/gates.md).

**Routes.** Taking a seasonal offer or renting a route makes the news when it is a new destination for the airline. Taking a seasonal offer on a route you already rent does not. It does not matter whether you have scheduled any flights on it yet.

**Fuel.** Every shock in the fuel price, up or down. The price is the same for every airline in the game, so the story is about the fuel market, not an airline. See [Shocks](../hq/fuel.md#shocks).

## Reading the news
Stories are listed newest first, under the day they happened. Each names the airline, and your own stories are tagged **You**. A fuel story reads **Fuel market** instead of an airline.

You can filter the list by airline (all airlines, yours, or your rivals) and by kind of story. Fuel stories show under all airlines only, never under yours or your rivals.

## What it does to you
Nothing more than what already happened. Being in the news does not cost money or reputation on its own: a Major incident already counts against your [reputation](../reputation.md), and making the news does not count it twice. Your rivals simply know.

Nothing keeps a story out of the news. A [Lawyer](../personnel/jobs/lawyer.md) reduces what an incident costs you, but not who hears about it.

## Your rivals
Until computer airlines or other players join your game, News shows only your own stories and the fuel market's.
