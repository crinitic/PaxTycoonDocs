# Flights
The **Flights** board is your schedule, one aircraft at a time, as a **Day** or a **Week**. Every time on it is on your home airport's clock.

## Map
The globe beside **Day | Week** opens a globe of your whole airline on one day: every route you hold, and every aircraft you own, where it is at the minute you pick. The map only shows. Nothing on it changes your schedule.

### What it shows
- **Your routes.** Every route you hold, rented or seasonal, as a line along the shortest way round the globe.
- **Ferry flights**, as a dashed line through their stops, on the days they fly.
- **Your fleet.** An aircraft in the air sits on its route, pointing the way it is flying. Aircraft on the ground share one disc per airport, with a count when there are two or more.
- **Your home airport**, as a star.
- **Your rivals' routes**, with an informer. **Rivals**, in the map's bottom-left corner, adds every route your rivals hold, in their own colour. A route you both fly shows your line edged in theirs. It shows the routes they hold, not their aircraft or which days they fly. The informer's lowest level is enough: see [Competitors](competitors.md).
- **Night**, shaded on the side of the globe away from the sun. It moves west as the day goes on. How many passengers want a departure depends on the local time where it leaves from, so this is where you see whose night it is.

Past days show what happened and future days show the plan. A cancelled flight never flew, so its aircraft stays on the ground.

### Time
The map opens on the board's day, or on today from the Week view, at 12:00 on your home clock.

- **The slider** moves the time through the day, from 00:00 to 24:00, five minutes at a time.
- **‹ and ›** page the day, from the game's first day to a week after today.
- **Play** runs the clock at one hour a second, so a day takes 24 seconds. At 24:00 it starts the same day again. Moving the slider or the day pauses it, and so does leaving the map.

### Turning and zooming
The globe opens with your home airport in the middle. Drag it to turn it and see the other side of the world, at any zoom. It turns all the way round east and west, and stops at the poles. Routes and aircraft on the far side aren't drawn until you turn the globe to them.

**+** and **−**, in the map's bottom-right corner, zoom from 1× to 16×, keeping whatever is in the middle where it is.

Aircraft and airports keep their size as you zoom, so zooming in pulls a busy airport apart. Paging the day and Play keep the zoom and the turn. Leaving the map takes it back to your home airport at 1×.

### Tapping
Tap an aircraft for its card: its name and type, the flight it is on or the next one it flies, and how long until it lands or departs. The card follows the aircraft while the clock runs, and stays on it when you page the day. **Open its board →** opens the Flights board on that aircraft.

Tap an airport with two or more aircraft on the ground for a list of them, then tap a name for its card.

Tap an empty part of the map, or the card's **×**, to close it.
