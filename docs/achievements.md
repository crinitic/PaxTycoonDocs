# Achievements
Achievements are how you earn development points (**DP**), and DP are how you unlock the rest of the game on your [development tree](development.md).

See them in the **Achievements** tab of the Development dialog, which you open from the menu at the top of the screen.

## How they work
**Achievements are checked every night**, after the night's money has been counted. Anything you did during the day earns its achievement the next morning, never straight away.

**They look at what you have, not what you did tonight.** If something is already true, it is earned on the next night, however long ago you did it. You never miss one by doing things in the wrong order.

**Each achievement pays once**, and each step of a ladder once. The morning report lists the achievements you earned overnight and the DP each paid, then how many DP you have to spend and what the cheapest node costs. **Development** there takes you to the tree.

Some achievements are about something you have to unlock first: you cannot build a lounge without Basic lounges. The Achievements tab tells you which node they need, for example **Needs Basic lounges**.

There are five kinds:

- **Basics**: one-offs that walk you through the game.
- **Progress**: ladders, which pay at every step, and pay more the higher you climb.
- **Skill**: playing well, whatever your size.
- **Exceptional**: the long game.
- **Secret**: hidden until you earn them.

## Progress
### Ladders
A ladder pays at each step it reaches: **5 DP** for the first step, **10** for the second, **15** for the third and **30** for the fourth. If you pass two steps on the same night, both pay.

The Achievements tab shows the step you have reached and how far you are from the next.

| Ladder | Counts | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Fleet size | Aircraft in your fleet | 5 / 25 / 100 / 250 |
| Employees | People on your staff, including anyone off sick or on a course | 50 / 250 / 1,000 |
| Destinations | Airports in this week's schedule | 10 / 50 / 100 |
| Direct from home | Airports this week's schedule flies to from home | 10 / 25 / 50 |
| Palace lounges | Lounges run at Palace level (needs Advanced lounges) | 5 / 25 |
| Passengers carried | Passengers flown since the start of the game | 1,000 / 100,000 / 1,000,000 / 10,000,000 |
| Offers completed | Seasonal offers flown in full | 5 / 25 / 100 |
| Reputation | Your [reputation](reputation.md) score | 25 / 50 / 75 |
| Net worth | Your balance and your fleet, less what you owe | ¤1bn / ¤10bn / ¤100bn |
| Years in business | Game years since the start | 1 / 3 / 5 |

**This week's schedule** is the flights of the next seven days, today's included. Only flights that carry passengers count: ferry flights and cancelled flights do not. Your home airport is never counted. So destinations measure where you fly now, not everywhere you have ever been.

**Net worth** is your balance, plus what your fleet would fetch if you sold it today, plus the fuel in your tanks at its average cost, less what you still owe on your loans.

### One-offs
Each pays **15 DP**.

| Achievement | Condition | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| In the black | A profitable 28 days | |
| Steady | Six profitable 28-day periods in a row | |
| Long lease | Keep a rented route for a full year | Direct rentals |
| New terminal | Open Terminal B | Terminals |
| Full range | Operate regional, narrowbody and widebody aircraft at the same time | Regional and Widebody |

A **28-day period** is a game month, and it is profitable when you earned more than you spent in it. Buying aircraft and fuel for your tanks does not count against it, and selling aircraft does not count towards it. A game year is 12 game months, 336 days.

## Skill
Each pays **20 DP**. Most need a minimum size, so a small airline cannot earn them by being small.

| Achievement | Condition | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Packed | 90% or more of your seats filled across your network for 28 days, with at least 20 aircraft | |
| Clockwork | No cancelled flights for 28 days, with at least 50 aircraft | |
| Loyal crew | Nobody quits for a year, with at least 250 staff | |
| Debt-free | No loans, with at least 50 aircraft | |
| Talent scout | Hire an Exceptional candidate | |
| Workhorse | One aircraft scheduled 18 hours or more in the air in one day | |
| Five stars | An all-3★ fleet of at least 50 aircraft | Premium service |
| Ultra long-haul | A route longer than 12,000 km in this week's schedule | Widebody |
| Paid for itself | What your tanks have saved adds up to more than you paid for them | Fuel management |
| Bought the dip | Your tanks hold at least 30 days of your schedule's fuel, at an average cost of ¤0.85 or less | Fuel management |
| Weathered the storm | On the night of an upward price shock, your whole fleet flies on fuel from your tanks, with at least 20 aircraft | Fuel management |

**Packed** and **Clockwork** also need something to fly on every one of the 28 days: an airline that flew nothing has no record to be proud of.

**Loyal crew** looks at the last year, so it cannot be earned before your first year in business. Firing does not count against it, only quitting.

**Talent scout** is about how good someone was when you hired them, not what training made of them later.

**Workhorse** counts the time in the air, ferry flights included. Time on the ground between flights does not count.

**Paid for itself** compares what your tank fuel cost with what the same fuel would have cost on the day it burned. It rewards a good **Use tank fuel** setting more than a big tank: see [What a tank can save](hq/fuel.md#what-a-tank-can-save).

**Weathered the storm** needs a shock, and every airline in the game sees the same ones, two or three a year. See [Shocks](hq/fuel.md#shocks).

## Secret
Secret achievements are not shown until you earn them. The Achievements tab only tells you how many are left to find. There are four, and each pays **5 DP**.

## In a game with everything unlocked
A game set up with **Everything unlocked** has no achievements and no DP: there is nothing left to unlock. See [Everything unlocked](development.md#everything-unlocked).
