# Personnel
## Pilot
Pilots fly the aircraft. Each aeroplane needs its full complement before it can go anywhere, and everybody in that complement has to be fit and on the job. One pilot short is a flight that does not depart, whatever else is ready.

### What competence changes
Competence here feeds feeds how often this person is the cause of an incident.

## Flight attendant
Attendants work the cabin, and how many an aeroplane needs depends on the aeroplane. Like pilots they are assigned to an aircraft, and like pilots a shortage grounds it.

### What competence changes
The same rule as a pilot's: nobody has modelled what a good attendant does for the cabin, only what a poor one might cause.
