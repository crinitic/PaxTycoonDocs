# Getting Started
## Your Airline
When you set up a game, you name your airline and its CEO. You can also give your CEO a face: pick their skin, hair, eyes, nose, glasses and mood yourself, or press **Randomize**. Until you do, your CEO is drawn as a silhouette. Every airline in the game sees both names and your CEO's face. You can change any of them at any time, as often as you like: click your airline's name at the top of the screen.

## What You Start With
Each airline starts with two airplanes (a321neo), one gate at your home airport, and the minimal personnel to operate your airline:

- 4 pilots and 10 flights attendants
- 1 mechanic
- 1 gate agent

Most of the game is locked at first. You unlock it as you go, with development points earned from [achievements](achievements.md). See [Development](development.md).

## Start Making Money
When you start a game, your airline is almost ready to fly. Your first action should be to get the rights to fly on a route. You start with **seasonal offers**:

- **A seasonal offer**: A seasonal offer lets you fly a route for 14, 28 or 56 days, for a fee you pay once, when you take it. Its days start the day after you take it, since today's flights are already locked, so a 28-day offer gives you 28 days to fly. Once its last day is over, you lose the right to fly this route. You can hold 3 seasonal offers at once. You can't take one on a route you already hold, by an offer or a rental.

Once you have unlocked **Direct rentals**, you can also rent a route:

- **A rented route**: A rented route never expires. Every day, a new set of passengers are willing to fly it. Every 28 days you must schedule at least 10% of the route's average monthly demand, the same number all year round (both directions count). If you don't, you lose the rented route (after a 28 days grace period) and cannot rent it again for 3 months.

Seasonal offers are cheaper, have a one-time cost, and have no penalty if not flown enough. An offer's fee is the same whatever the season, so an offer that runs through a route's busy months is a bargain, and one through its quiet months is not. Rented routes are more expensive, have a recurring cost, and are lost if they are not used enough.

Either way, you share a route's passengers with every other airline flying it. Holding a seasonal offer does not keep them for you.

A seasonal offer is a good way to try a route out. If it works, rent it: you can rent a route you hold an offer on, and the rental takes over its flights straight away.

## Your First Achievements
Your first few nights pay for your first few unlocks. Fly a flight, take a seasonal offer, hire someone, buy or sell an aircraft: each of these is a basics achievement worth 3 development points (DP), paid the next morning. The morning report tells you what you earned and what you can buy with it. See [Basics](achievements.md#basics).

## What You Should Get Early
### Personnel Advisor
The personnel advisor is going to help you decide who to hire next. See more in [Personnel Advisor](personnel/jobs/personnel-advisor.md).

### Route Advisor
The route advisor is going to help you decide where to fly, and when. See more in [Route Advisor](personnel/jobs/route-advisor.md).

### Your First Nodes
The cheapest nodes on the [development tree](development.md) are the ones most airlines want first:

- **Extra gates** (5 DP). You start with one gate, so only one aircraft at a time can turn around at home. See [Gates](airport/gates.md).
- **Security manager** (10 DP), before you buy aircraft. A good security manager can help you save a lot of money when it matters most. See more in [Security Manager](personnel/jobs/security-manager.md).
- **Direct rentals** (15 DP), for routes that never expire.

There is no order you have to follow. Buy what suits the airline you want to run.

## Your Schedule
Every day in the game runs on your home airport's clock, the same clock your flight board shows: today's flights are locked and fly tonight, and you can plan the next seven days. A flight belongs to the day it departs on at home, even if the local date where it takes off is different.

## Everything Unlocked
If your game was set up with **Everything unlocked**, there are no achievements and no development tree: you can rent routes, hire a security manager and use everything else from your first day. See [Everything unlocked](development.md#everything-unlocked).
